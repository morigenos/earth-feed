# Decisions

Append one line per decision, newest at the bottom, with the reason.

- 2026-09-17 Natural Earth for boundaries: public domain, and it maps de facto control with disputed lines kept separate. The app says so rather than presenting any line as settled.
- 2026-09-17 The claude.ai page cannot reach the network. Confirmed against runtime contract 0.2.52: no capability grants egress. Live data therefore lives in the downloaded copy or in data Claude writes into the artifact database.
- 2026-09-17 Blitzortung rejected for lightning: participant-only raw data, commercial use barred, redistribution requires your own server. GOES GLM chosen instead, through the feed.
- 2026-09-18 satellite.js 7 with OMM rather than legacy TLE: CelesTrak catalogue numbers have outgrown the two-line format.
- 2026-09-18 CelesTrak refreshed no more often than every 6 hours, per their usage policy.
- 2026-09-18 Pipelines blocked: Global Energy Monitor requires a request form. Submarine cables blocked: the public TeleGeography repository is gone.
- 2026-09-19 Deep Time uses published GPlates models (Merdith 2021, Müller 2022, Cao 2024). Anything older than a model's range is drawn as an explicitly illustrative scene, never as a reconstruction.
- 2026-09-20 Feed health is authoritative for layer status: `health.json` decides whether a layer reads as live, unconfigured or failed.
- 2026-10-10 Fuel prices start with the EU Weekly Oil Bulletin: free, reproduction authorised with acknowledgement, prices with and without taxes, weekly history since 2005. History is rebuilt from the Commission's workbook on every download rather than archived here, so a lost gh-pages branch loses nothing. Countries are keyed by ISO alpha-3.
- 2026-10-10 The gh-pages restore now fails the run unless the branch is restored or truly absent, and publishing requires a successful restore: before, a failed fetch fell back to main's frozen data and force-pushed it over the live site.
- 2026-10-10 One globe build: the Stage 1–3 build is the source of truth for the offline copy, `index.html` and the claude.ai page; the coral layer, feed-health list and Pages feed address were ported into it.
- 2026-10-10 Fuel beyond the EU uses only official open sources: US EIA (public domain), UK DESNZ (Open Government Licence v3.0), Statistics Canada (Open Licence), NZ MBIE (CC BY 4.0), Malaysia data.gov.my (CC BY 4.0). GlobalPetrolPrices stays out until its licence terms are confirmed in writing; goriva.si data is for personal use and never enters this feed.
- 2026-10-10 Each country's petrol map shows its standard grade (US and Canadian regular, New Zealand 91) with the grade named, rather than forcing a 95 RON comparison those sources do not publish.
- 2026-10-10 The UK comes from DESNZ's weekly series, not the EU bulletin, which stopped at the end of 2020; the bulletin collector skips GBR so the two never overwrite each other.
- 2026-10-10 Station prices for France, Spain and Italy, coloured by rank within each area rather than by euro value: a national euro scale hides the local spread that makes a station cheap or dear. Spain's Canary Islands and Ceuta and Melilla are ranked separately because their taxes differ.
- 2026-10-10 France's station timestamps are Paris local time despite a UTC label; the collector reads them as Europe/Paris after checking them against the fetch time on GitHub's runners.
- 2026-10-10 Spain's station service (sedeaplicaciones.minetur.gob.es) resets connections from most GitHub runner addresses (five of five attempts in one run, three runs out of four on 10 Oct); its geoportal's bulk download needs a login. The collector keeps retrying each run and the globe keeps Spain's last stations, labelled with their date, until a run gets through.
- 2026-10-10 Mexico added from the Comisión Nacional de Energía's daily price and station lists. The CNE page names no licence; the dataset is catalogued on datos.gob.mx, whose federal open-data terms (Libre Uso MX, CC BY-compatible) require citing the agency, dataset, link and consultation date, which the attribution does. Prices stay in pesos with the ECB euro rate beside them, so nothing is lost to conversion.
- 2026-10-10 Not added: Argentina (CC BY 4.0, but only 35 of 1,561 YPF stations reported a price in the last 30 days, so a ranking would be skewed); Portugal (complete, current data, but the DGEG publishes no reuse terms); UK Fuel Finder (needs GOV.UK One Login registration and returns 403 to GitHub's runners); Germany (Tankerkönig, CC BY 4.0, needs an API key from the owner).
- 2026-10-10 Portugal re-checked: the DGEG price portal's footer says the information is free and may be used freely, but not for commercial purposes. That is not an open licence, so Portugal stays out unless Archie accepts a non-commercial source on the public feed.
- 2026-10-10 US and Canadian regions are coloured against the country's own figure (±20%, blue cheaper, red dearer) when zoomed in: on the world price scale the spread inside one country was a few shades of the same orange. Change metrics keep the world scale, which is already relative.
- 2026-10-10 Canadian provinces show the plain average of their Statistics Canada cities rather than a weighted one: there are no published weights, and the panel lists the cities so nothing is hidden.
- 2026-10-10 EIA's regional workbooks are fetched six at a time and only once per published week: one at a time took 10.6 minutes on a runner, over the step's limit.
